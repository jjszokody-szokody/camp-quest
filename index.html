❓ How to Play

🎯 Your goal

Build a camp around your campfire, survive hunger, thirst, cold and heat, explore four realms — ❄️ Snow (north), 🌴 Jungle (east), 🏜️ Desert (south), 🌋 Volcano (west) — and defeat the four bosses: the Yeti King, Vine Queen, Sand Pharaoh and Magma Titan. Beat all four to win the Crown of the Four Realms.

❤️ Surviving

Watch the bars (top-left): Health, Hunger, Thirst, Temperature and the clock (top-centre, with the day number). Eat food and drink at ponds/wells (fill a waterskin). Snow drains temperature without cold gear; desert and lava drain it without heat gear. Days last about 5 minutes. It gets dark late in the evening — at 18:00 your lights switch on and your campfire circle turns day-bright (see below) before true night falls.

🎮 Controls

Computer: WASD/arrows move · mouse click = hit (aims at cursor) · right-click/F = shoot bow · Space = jump · E = use/talk (hold E on a placed item to pick it up and move it) · B = break a placed item next to you for a material refund (full refund if undamaged, half if damaged; loaded turret arrows are returned too — your main campfire, an upgraded bench or a leveled-up campfire asks you to press B twice to confirm) · Q or hold shield + right-click = block · R = rotate a building while placing/moving · 1–9 hotbar (press again to use/place) · Tab/I bag · C recipe book · L quest log · J/K hit · M map · wheel zoom · right-drag or Z/X rotate camera · Esc pause/close.

Phone: left joystick to move; HIT, USE, JUMP, SHOOT, EAT, CRAFT buttons; tap a hotbar slot to select, tap again to use/place; ROTATE, BLOCK, PICK UP / PLACE / PUT BACK and BREAK buttons appear when you need them (BREAK refunds a placed item's materials — tap twice to confirm for your main fire or upgraded bench/fire).

⛏️ Gathering rules

Trees take 10 hits with fists or 5 with any axe. Rocks and ores need a pickaxe: stone pick for rock, then iron→gold→obsidian tiers for iron, gold/crystal and obsidian. Punch bushes and pebbles for fibre, berries and stone.

🛠️ Crafting, benches & the fire

Your bag has a 2×2 crafting grid; a crafting bench gives 3×3 and unlocks higher recipes. Upgrade the bench through tiers 1–4 for iron, gold and obsidian gear. The recipe book shows every pattern — Auto-fill loads the grid, and Craft all (or double-click/double-tap a recipe) makes as many as you can afford.

Use E at your campfire to feed it logs and upgrade it (levels 1–5: stone ring → hearth → iron brazier → bonfire → great beacon) for a bigger safe radius, more fuel, slower burning, healing and soldier-slowing perks. The blue circle is the safe area: monsters stay out, and from 18:00 until dawn the ground inside it is lit as bright as day — outside stays night-dark. Add logs before it dies!

🏘️ Villages, quests & shops

Five villages have houses, wells, gardens and shops. Talk to villagers (E): they give side quests (❗/❓ markers), rumours that reveal boss locations on your map, and heart pieces (4 = +20 max health). Shopkeepers trade for coins — sell hides, ores and gems.

👑 Bosses & treasure

Each boss guards its realm; get its location from village rumours or the quest log before you go. Boss fights have red warning circles — step out of them! Bosses drop trophies for the best gear. Look for sparkling treasure chests (about 38, richest near bosses and in dangerous realms): open with E for ingots, gems, coins, rare gear and occasionally a heart piece. Found chests appear on your map.

🥁 Forest Soldier raids

On the night of day 5, 10, 15… Forest Soldiers attack — one more soldier each raid. The HUD counts down ("Raid in N nights"). They come for you, not your fire. Build defences: spike barricades, walls and gates, turrets (load them with arrows), traps, tar pits, trenches, a watchtower, an alarm bell and a scarecrow decoy — craft them at the bench and repair them with E.

🧺 Wandering traders

On even days (2, 4, 6…) Fern the Forest Trader arrives in the morning (06:00) and stays camped by your campfire all day until the next dawn — a full 24-hour visit (lantern + bedroll, 🧺 on the map, listen for her bell). She sleeps late at night (💤 Zzz) but you can still wake her to trade. She buys meat, hides, wood, stone and berries at fair prices, with Sell-all. Every 3 days in daytime Bram the Defense Trader (🛡️) parks his cart by your campfire for the day and sells barricades, walls, turrets and arrow bundles — plus two trader-only specials: the Thorn wall and the Guardian totem (slows enemies nearby), and the stronger Guardian shield. Fern stays all day, though Bram still leaves at dusk — trade with him while he’s here!

👤 Make it yours

Open Character on the title screen or in pause to set your name, skin, hair, outfit, face and hat (boss and quest hats unlock as you play). Armour you equip still shows on top.
